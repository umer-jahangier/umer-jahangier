## Direction contract

THESIS: The profile is a cyclorama: a stage lit from night to day, where light reveals a year of mostly private work. It refuses both the neon-terminal dev profile and the flat stat-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Dark theme is the night cue: depthless black sky, a low cobalt horizon (#2A3DE0 to #5A76FF) with rose (#FF6F91) gathering above it, stars. Light theme is the day cue: white wash, rose haze at the horizon, cobalt architecture. Archivo expanded for the name and figures. Cards are dark stages (#080A15) or white (#FFFFFF), 18px radius, hairline edges. Private = warm windows and solid chips; public = cool windows and outlined chips.

STORY: The visitor sees who and what (name, role), then the scale of the year as a city whose lit windows are mostly private, then when the work happens (sun path), in what (sky strata), what is live now (call sheet), the ledger, the named work with each project's own silhouette, and contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width hero stage: name in one expanded line across the sky, role and credit line beneath, and an isometric skyline of 52 weekly buildings standing on the horizon with its reflection in the ground plane. A sun (day) or moon (night) sits in the sky where the name ends. Contact buttons directly below. Below the hero the stage continues: asymmetric bento rows (700/460, then 460/700) whose cards carry the theme's horizon haze.

FORM: Cyclorama (bolder re-roll, round 1, seed key 56d09fd9), fused with the minihompy room's isometric depth and the overprint discipline of private/public layers. Signature motion: window lights and stars twinkle, horizon glow breathes; every loop starts from the finished frame.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
